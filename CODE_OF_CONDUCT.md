# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss the code, behavior, and evidence rather than attacking the person reporting a problem or proposing a change. Welcome newcomers, give useful reproduction details, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Don't post private transcripts, credentials, or personal screenshots without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation to keep the project useful and welcoming. If an issue involves private or safety-sensitive information, don't post the details publicly; request a private contact channel or use GitHub's report-abuse tools.
