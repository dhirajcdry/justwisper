What it does

Dictation, done right.

Fast, private, and native — the way a Mac tool should feel.

01

On-device, always

Whisper runs on the Apple Neural Engine. Works on a plane, offline, wherever — your audio never touches a server.

02

One key, two ways

Hold Right ⌥ to talk and release. Double-tap to latch hands-free. Esc cancels — and even a cancel can be undone.

03

Sets itself as you speak

Fillers and stutters gone, casing and punctuation fixed, your names and jargon spelled the way you mean.

04

Lands in any app

Pastes right at your cursor in Mail, Slack, Xcode, your browser — then a tap re-copies it anywhere with ⌘V.

05

Knows the room

Per-app styles: loose and casual in Slack, buttoned-up in Mail, plain in your editor. Pin whatever you like.

06

Yours to keep

Local history, insights on your speaking, and a one-click erase. It's your data, on your Mac, full stop.

Privacy

Your voice is nobody's business.

Most dictation apps stream your microphone to the cloud. justwisper doesn't — and can't. The model lives on your Mac, the transcription happens on your Mac, and the text goes straight into your app. There's no server to trust because there's no server at all.

No cloud

Audio is captured and transcribed entirely on-device via the Apple Neural Engine.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. No email, no login, no analytics, no telemetry.

No lock-in

Open source, MIT-licensed. History is a local file you can read — or erase in one click.

Get it

Talk to your Mac.

Download, drag to Applications, grant two permissions, and hold Right ⌥. That's the whole setup.
